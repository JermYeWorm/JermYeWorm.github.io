Hi everyone! I'm an electrical engineer with a strong interest in biomedical engineering and entrepreneurship. This website includes some projects to highlight my previous work.

My core technical interests include wearable biosensors and interfaces, analog and mixed-signal IC design, and real-time embedded ML algorithms for physiological monitoring. I have a background with end-to-end engineering across diverse domains, including medical device development, semiconductor chip design, and spacecraft systems. I'm also interested in translating cutting-edge research into practical solutions, with experience in technology commercialization, customer discovery, translational grants, and clinical collaborations.

Lately, I have been working full time to commercialize my research on a noninvasive adhesive smartpatch for real-time vascular flow monitoring, built to automatically track vascular access dysfunction in dialysis patients. Learn more about this work [here](https://youtu.be/ZMXzoks5MnI).

In my free time, I enjoy golf, cooking, world history, mountains, and juggling!
